# 💸 BudgetZ — Gen Z Financial Simulator

> **Survive the Month & Master Your Bag.**  
> A modern, interactive consumer-facing personal finance experience built to simulate real-world financial decisions over a 30-day budget cycle.

---

## 🌟 Overview

**BudgetZ** goes beyond basic expense logging by connecting **income, balance, spending, commitments, savings goals, subscriptions, unexpected expenses, financial risk, and financial health**. 

Built with a light, clean aesthetic and zero prebuilt dummy data, BudgetZ allows users to simulate financial choices before making them, providing real-time feedback on how every rupee impacts their 30-day trajectory.

---

## ⚡ Key Features

* 🛠️ **Custom Setup Engine**: Define your starting income/budget (baseline ₹10,000) and target savings goals with clean slate parameters.
* 🔮 **Interactive Decision Simulator**: Sandbox purchases *before* spending to forecast post-decision balance, daily allowance, and health score impact.
* 🧾 **Commitments & Subscriptions Tracker**: Log fixed recurring bills (Rent, WiFi, Netflix) to maintain an accurate monthly burn rate.
* 🚨 **Unexpected Expense Shock Generator**: Simulate real-life financial emergencies (Medical, Repairs, Home Shock) with custom cost overrides.
* 📅 **Custom 30-Day Calendar Picker**: Allocate transactions across a clean, custom 30-day timeline.
* 📈 **Live Trajectory Analytics**: Interactive Chart.js charts showing balance trajectory vs. savings thresholds and category spending breakdowns.
* 🔍 **AI Financial Audit**: Perform live health checks to identify overspending categories and evaluate overall financial risk.

---

## 🚀 Tech Stack

* **Framework**: [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router, JavaScript/JSX)
* **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
* **Analytics & Charts**: [Chart.js](https://www.chartjs.org/) & `react-chartjs-2`
* **Icons & Aesthetics**: Glassmorphism UI with Tailwind Light Blue / Emerald palette
